The engineering standard

Liquid cooling is the product.

Frontier AI racks are not an air-cooled proposition. Our shells are engineered around the liquid loop from day one — that is what 2.5 MW per acre means, and why the densest AI hardware lands without compromise. The platform is Rubin-ready: structured for smooth migration from GB200-class to Rubin Ultra-class hardware without re-plumbing the hall.

2.5

2.5 MW per acre

Ten-acre estates carrying 25 MW each — density that air cooling cannot reach. Compact campuses mean shorter pipe runs, tighter electrical topology, and land costs that round to zero in the capital stack.

CDU

Direct-to-chip, factory-integrated

The liquid plant ships in the module: direct-to-chip and rear-door heat exchange with smart CDUs, warm-water loops, quick-connects, and leak protection — base building, not tenant fit-out. Liquid-ready for 250 kW-class racks, engineered to a 1.10 design-point PUE.

Prefabricated, factory-integrated modules — power, cooling, networking — land on site tested. Twenty identical phases across four sites turn construction from a project into a production line, with delivery windows committed at contract.
